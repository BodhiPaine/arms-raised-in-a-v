I got a passport photo today because I am heading to Malaysia, where I hope to ordain as a [#Buddhist](https://mastodon.social/tags/Buddhist) [#monk](https://mastodon.social/tags/monk). I say this out loud even though most of my people know this plan ~ for those of you who don't: such is my plan. 

The last time I had a passport issued was 2011, and it expired in 2021. I was a whole-different person then ~ I was still working in publishing, and that whole time feels like a past life now, has felt like that for maybe a decade.

Time and identity are such fluid things.
