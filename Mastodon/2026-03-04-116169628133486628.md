An Aeon essay through one of my favourite subjects: subversive literature, and whether it is actually subversive, by Charlie Ericson.

[https://aeon.co/essays/feel-the-burn-how-subversion-works-in-literature](https://aeon.co/essays/feel-the-burn-how-subversion-works-in-literature)

He discusses [#Dante](https://mastodon.social/tags/Dante) and [#Milton](https://mastodon.social/tags/Milton), and a contemporary poet, Shane McCrae, and makes a distinction between subversion and [#revolution](https://mastodon.social/tags/revolution). 

It go me thinking about how literature's role in renewing [#perception](https://mastodon.social/tags/perception) might transcend both of these.
