Mushrooms lifting a metal strip off the ground! The strip is screwed into the concrete on the left, and to the right outside the frame! 

I learnt reading Robin Wall [#Kimmerer](https://mastodon.social/tags/Kimmerer) ‘s book Democracy of Species that the Anishinaabemowin (Ojibwe) word “puhpowee” refers to “the force which causes mushrooms to push up from the earth overnight”. 

What [#puhpowee](https://mastodon.social/tags/puhpowee) these soft beings have! After they save our minds from the [#apocalypse](https://mastodon.social/tags/apocalypse) they will reclaim the land from cities!
