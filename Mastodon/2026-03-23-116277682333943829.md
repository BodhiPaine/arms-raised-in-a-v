eBook platforms should have a DRM setting (if they don’t already) where I can assign my ownership to a friend, like I would pass a book on once finished. Publishers used to (and maybe still do) extrapolate from direct sales to actual readership by an order of 3, assuming that people who bought and liked a book would pass it on to friends. 

I don’t much like the idea of enjoying a novel and then it just sits in the background of my eReader forever.
