The article doesn’t wipe [#trauma](https://mastodon.social/tags/trauma) off the table, but accounts for the origins of [#neurodivergence](https://mastodon.social/tags/neurodivergence), about which I have wondered for a long time. 

Importantly, the complexity-science approach makes room for what also has seemed obvious to me: neurodivergence is not a [#pathology](https://mastodon.social/tags/pathology), but an advantage.

Less obvious to me but something emerging in my thinking about this is the need for neurodivergence-informed language and treatment approaches the same as we have trauma-informed awareness. 2/n 🧵
