In Buddhism there is a term, chanda, which loosely means “wholesome ambition” or “wholesome desire”. Whereas desire in the form of taṇhā is a cause of suffering, chanda is a form of desire that moves us away from suffering, toward enlightenment, which is why it is considered wholesome.

This whole mission and vision is characterised by chanda, and I love that.  🧵 4/5
