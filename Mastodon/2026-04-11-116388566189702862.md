I've been [#bushwalking](https://mastodon.social/tags/bushwalking) off-path lately and enjoying how I've needed to renounce [#conditioning](https://mastodon.social/tags/conditioning) to do so: in [#Australia](https://mastodon.social/tags/Australia) there is almost a taboo against leaving the ordained path, and the state is the church in this sense. 

Going off-path in the backyards of some large properties I've had the fortune to live on means I get to traverse passages of land that are inhabited more by animal-animals than by humanimals and it feels ... different, nice. 1/n 🧵
