I loved this [#film](https://mastodon.social/tags/film), Perfect Days, depicting the especially ordinary Hirayama as he cleans Tokyo's extraordinary public toilets by day, and reads classic Western [#literature](https://mastodon.social/tags/literature) by night. He also listens to [#casettes](https://mastodon.social/tags/casettes) and takes aimless photos of tree canopies.

[https://www.sbs.com.au/ondemand/movie/perfect-days/2479790147928](https://www.sbs.com.au/ondemand/movie/perfect-days/2479790147928)

The film inspired me to up-notch my appreciation of ordinariness, and caused me to wonder why I am so obsessed with achieving great things.

Have you seen it?
