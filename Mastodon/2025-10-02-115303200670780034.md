Collecting and processing notes about a long walking [#pilgrimage](https://mastodon.social/tags/pilgrimage) for a [#memoir](https://mastodon.social/tags/memoir) essay is much more overwhelming than I thought it would be.

Does anyone have any tips? 

Merely remembering that I need to make notes is a huge shift in my habitual way of being, and then there is a lot involved in processing those notes. 

I dictate into my phone while walking for 1.5 hours and wind up with 6000 words of garbled stream of consciousness!
