Today I embark on my first solo outdoor adventure since I got married nearly ten years ago. Nine days [#hiking](https://mastodon.social/tags/hiking) in the Grampians ⛰️😍

I just realised this is indeed the context for this trip. I really lost myself in that marriage, and consequently for years my outlook on horizons and potential was like through this window.
 
Lack of connection with self and [#Nature](https://mastodon.social/tags/Nature) really is a cause of personal and spiritual bankruptcy. But today I feel like Milo from The Phantom Tollbooth. Hallelujah 🙏🏼
