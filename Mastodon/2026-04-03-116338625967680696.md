I don’t like the militant-activist/conspiracy-theorist [#rhetoric](https://mastodon.social/tags/rhetoric) that “if you’re not part of the solution you’re part of the problem”. What if you’re busy managing a young family, or chronic health problems, or are just crumpled by despair? 

For some it is hard enough to get your own house in order, and doing so is a form of [#resistance](https://mastodon.social/tags/resistance) against the economic forces that keep the underclass alienated from more-#direct forms of [#action](https://mastodon.social/tags/action).
