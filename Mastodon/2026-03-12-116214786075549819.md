What are your strategies for protecting your attention span among the mauling clutches of the [#attention](https://mastodon.social/tags/attention) [#economy](https://mastodon.social/tags/economy)? Without abandoning [#social](https://mastodon.social/tags/social) [#media](https://mastodon.social/tags/media) and [#AI](https://mastodon.social/tags/AI) altogether. 

These technologies are here to stay and their impacts are not all bad. Abandoning them would be a serious case of throwing the baby out with the bathwater. 

The socials are not the problem anymore than any other external circumstance is the problem - our relationship with these "problems" is the problem.
