In the Mahāparinibbāna Sutta and elsewhere in the Buddha's teaching, 

[https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato](https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato)

memorisation is important to the practice. The teachings were originally oral, and had to be memorised for transmission. 

Something might have been lost when things were written down, and there might be something to regain through resuming a [#practice](https://mastodon.social/tags/practice) of internalising the teachings so that we can take them everywhere, not having outsourced their storage to [#technology](https://mastodon.social/tags/technology).
