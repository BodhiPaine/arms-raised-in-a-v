The most-valuable idea I have got from this book so far is that [#consciousness](https://mastodon.social/tags/consciousness) could be an [#emergent](https://mastodon.social/tags/emergent) [#property](https://mastodon.social/tags/property) of [#matter](https://mastodon.social/tags/matter) in the same way magnetic fields, for example, are an emergent property. I tend to lean toward [#Idealism](https://mastodon.social/tags/Idealism), in terms of what I would \*like to believe\*, but as a [#dhamma](https://mastodon.social/tags/dhamma) practitioner I also appreciate the teachings of [#dependent](https://mastodon.social/tags/dependent) [#origination](https://mastodon.social/tags/origination), which says that mind and matter (namarupa) arise in dependence on each other and other phenomena. 1/2
