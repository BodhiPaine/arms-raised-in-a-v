I was listening to a podcast yesterday and couldn’t finish it because the guy sounded like he was trying to be Terrence McKenna. Then this morning I was working on a philosophical travel [#memoir](https://mastodon.social/tags/memoir) and realised I was trying to emulate Robert Pirsig 🤔

I appreciate that all [#art](https://mastodon.social/tags/art) is derivative and it’s fine to be inspired by the greats but the real hack is taking inspiration into a voice and form of one’s own. 

Reminds me of that book Steal Like an Artist. A perennial problem, no doubt. [#amWriting](https://mastodon.social/tags/amWriting)
