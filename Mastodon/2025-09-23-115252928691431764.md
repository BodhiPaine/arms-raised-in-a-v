We hear a lot that wanting validation through likes on our posts and whatnot is unhealthy. 

It’s the whole dopamine thing right? 

We refresh the screen to see if we’ve got any more likes and we get a little dopamine hit if we do ~ that’s one part of it. 

The other part is that we get to feel seen. We get to know that our friends care about what we’re doing, and that’s healthy, and that’s okay. 

As with many things, it can be addictive and maladaptive, or it can be healthy and affirming.
