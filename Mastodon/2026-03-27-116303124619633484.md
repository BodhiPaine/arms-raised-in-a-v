Try not spending [#money](https://mastodon.social/tags/money) for three months. I don’t mean just pay the bills and don’t buy anything nice. I mean, stay in a hermitage or something if you can, and maintain a resolution to not spend any money. It’s harder than it sounds. 

We have been so conditioned to believe that our worth is dependent on how much and what we consume that I have experienced low self-worth and even depressive symptoms over the years at times when I wasn’t able to spend money. 🤔
