I love that Charles [#Eisenstein](https://mastodon.social/tags/Eisenstein) shares hopeful narratives about our times when it seems almost taboo to entertain a brighter future today. Such a future depends on stating this possibility. 

The world’s ills begin and end with taking responsibility for our own maligned interiors. Such a position requires confidence in a perspective much larger than [#modernity](https://mastodon.social/tags/modernity) views generally afford, and the courage to advocate radical responsibility.
