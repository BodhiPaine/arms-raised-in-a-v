I am glad to have had [@organicmaps](https://fosstodon.org/@organicmaps) recommended to me recently, and immediately made good use of it when I was [#hiking](https://mastodon.social/tags/hiking) on the Sunny Coast last weekend. 

Being able to see my location alongside both [#topographical](https://mastodon.social/tags/topographical) info and nearby walking trails meant that my friend and I could discern confidently that, yes, that hill up ahead is where we were going ~ so we could psych ourselves up for the climb. 

Love ya work, oMaps!
