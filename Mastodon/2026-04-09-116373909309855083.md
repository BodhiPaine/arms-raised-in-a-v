I was raised on [#hedonism](https://mastodon.social/tags/hedonism) and old habits die hard. Sometimes I indulge, though I know it’s a fool’s game. I have “junkie-reasoning” for that indulgence, which is that [#abstinence](https://mastodon.social/tags/abstinence) robs us of the opportunity to parse the folly of pursuing [#happiness](https://mastodon.social/tags/happiness) through [#pleasure](https://mastodon.social/tags/pleasure). 

It’s not water-tight, this “argument”, but it’s predicated on the [#assumption](https://mastodon.social/tags/assumption) that we can’t transform our [#attachment](https://mastodon.social/tags/attachment) to what we are not in [#contact](https://mastodon.social/tags/contact) with. 

Happy to discuss ~ eager, even: I could be wrong… it’s happened before!
