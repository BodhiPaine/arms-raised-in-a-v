Reflections from [@buddhistdoor](https://mastodon.social/@buddhistdoor) about how embracing [#uncertainty](https://mastodon.social/tags/uncertainty) (during these times that seem increasingly unstable) is not passive or submissive, but when combined with other [#Buddhist](https://mastodon.social/tags/Buddhist) principles leaves us open to new possibilities to co-create a more-harmonious world.

Considering reality is inherently [#impermanent](https://mastodon.social/tags/impermanent), 
accepting uncertainty seems like the only sane way to approach things. [#Attachment](https://mastodon.social/tags/Attachment) to certainty causes only [#suffering](https://mastodon.social/tags/suffering) and yet more conflict.
