As a [#dhamma](https://mastodon.social/tags/dhamma) practitioner in recovery from complex trauma, I am also aware caffeine stimulates the SNS and is therefore counter-productive in the pursuit of [#samadhi](https://mastodon.social/tags/samadhi) and [#insight](https://mastodon.social/tags/insight). 

Such insight and the perils of capitalism are not independent concerns. By cultivating wisdom to undermine our own sense desire (described by Buddha as the fuel for suffering), we reduce the fuel supplied to the desire machines of capitalism. 

We cannot be content and endlessly productive in the same breath. 2/3 🧵
